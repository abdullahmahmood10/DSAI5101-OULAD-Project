Do early assessment scores help?

Removing the two score features reduced CatBoost’s 2014J ROC-AUC from 0.7772 to 0.7570. At their respective development-tuned thresholds, the share flagged rose from 54.1% to 61.4%.

The score-free model is relevant when grades may be unavailable by day 27. Use flags to prioritise supportive outreach.
